How it works

BALCAM uses WebRTC for direct device-to-device media when the network allows it. An internet-based signalling service helps devices connect. Network restrictions may prevent connections. Video is not intentionally uploaded to a video-storage server.

Keep your room code private. Only share it with people you trust. Recordings are saved separately on each camera device.
